🚗🇦🇺 Australia Car Market Data
This repository contains a comprehensive dataset on the Australian car market, providing insights into car prices, features, 
sales trends, and market demand. This dataset is ideal for data analysts, car buyers, sellers, and market researchers looking 
to explore the trends in car pricing, vehicle specifications, and consumer preferences in Australia.

📌 Features
📊 Dataset Overview
Car Details: Make, model, variant, year of manufacture.
Pricing Information: New and used car prices.
Vehicle Specifications: Engine type, fuel type, transmission, drivetrain.
Mileage & Fuel Efficiency: Average fuel consumption, kilometers driven.
Body Type: Sedan, SUV, hatchback, coupe, etc.
Safety Ratings: ANCAP crash test ratings.
Seller Type: Dealer, private seller, certified pre-owned.
Location Information: State-wise car listings in Australia.
